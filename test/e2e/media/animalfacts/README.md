# shorts

The videos of our YouTube channel Animal Facts: vertical shorts of about 30 seconds, each one surprising, true fact about one animal, spoken by a calm, friendly narrator.

The whole channel has one look, so a viewer knows it at a glance: soft, flat illustrations with warm colours and a plain light background, the animal in the middle. The shorts are made of video clips only, no still pictures.

For children and families: friendly, never scary or gory.
